Watch YouTube videos right inside Figma.

Keep a tutorial, design talk, or reference video beside your work without switching tabs to watch. YoutubeWatch brings a compact, resizable video player into your Figma workspace.

Features
- Play, pause, and seek from a compact control bar.
- Adjust volume or mute with one click.
- Fit the window to the video's proportions.
- Hide the link toolbar while watching and bring it back when you need it.
- Resize the player to suit your workspace.
- Open standard YouTube links, youtu.be links, Shorts, live videos, and video IDs.
- Start from the timestamp in your video link.

How to use
1. Run YoutubeWatch in a Figma Design file.
2. Paste a YouTube link and press Enter.
3. Press Play, adjust the size, and keep designing.

An internet connection is required. Some private, age-restricted, or embedding-disabled videos may not play.
